# Scientific data and image use

The software MIT license does not grant reuse rights to scientific staining images, public preview images, anatomical image layers, intensity arrays, model bundles or lab-supplied visual guidance. Public viewing and password-authorized original downloads are access arrangements, not a blanket scientific-data license. Request reuse permission from the relevant data owners through the project maintainer: https://github.com/huangmengxuan351-cpu/bicyclus-wing-atlas/issues . Do not post private specimens or credentials in public issues.

Additional HH staining photographs received in October 2026 are visual guidance only. They are not new atlas samples, model inputs or a basis for fabricated fluorescence intensities. The released atlas remains at 12 genes, 291 image units, 72 groups and 216 processing results until a separately reviewed data release is approved.
